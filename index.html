Garage gym

Strength for cross-country skiing. Kettlebells, dumbbells, BOSU. Everything runs on a timer, just put the phone down next to you.

A arms, chest, core, light legsB legs, triceps, some coreLong ~41–42 min · Short ~30 min
  • Every exercise: name, get ready, 3 s, GO, countdown, STOP. The last 5 seconds are counted out loud: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1. Single-arm and single-leg exercises run as two exercises with a 15 s rest between sides.
  • Each pair works different muscles. 15 s rest between exercises, 60 s (Long) or 45 s (Short) after each round.
  • Rest: “rest 15”, then 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 out loud and straight into the next exercise.
  • Space or → skips ahead, ← goes back, P pauses. Use Short when recovery is below 40 % or time is tight.
